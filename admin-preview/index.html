Feature example

Provision recruiter

Admin creates a recruiter with a Medlivo email, role, team and active status. Google identity must bind before access is granted.

Deactivate account

Admin disables access without deleting operational history. Audit retains who changed the account and when.

Security rule

A company email address alone never grants access. Provisioning + approved Google identity + RBAC are required.
