# ADR 002: Role and Rate Ownership

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-10-10

## Decision

### Executive

Owns company-wide strategic visibility, major exceptions, and negative-GM exception decisions.

### Delivery Manager

Owns operational control of managed teams, assignment, priority, intake, workload, start risk, and guidance when recruiter escalation is needed.

### Recruiter

Owns recruiting execution and final rate decisions within company guidelines.

Normal rate finalization is not a manager approval workflow.

### System Admin

Is a capability, not a higher business hierarchy. System Admin controls configuration, access, teams, integrations, and governed reference data.

## Gross-margin decision rule

- within guideline: recruiter finalizes
- discuss Delivery Manager: recruiter records consultation and finalizes
- discuss leadership: recruiter records consultation and finalizes
- negative GM: Executive hard exception required
- unconfigured policy: fail closed

## Consequence

Do not convert ordinary recruiter rate decisions into routine manager approvals.

Any change requires a superseding ADR and explicit approval.
