# ADR 003: AI and Human Decision Boundary

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-10-10

## Decision

AI may:

- extract
- normalize
- classify
- summarize
- detect gaps/conflicts
- draft
- explain
- rank
- recommend
- prioritize

Humans retain consequential decision authority unless a later approved policy explicitly delegates a bounded automation.

## Required safeguards

- AI must not invent unsupported candidate, job, credential, schedule, rate, availability, malpractice, procedure, or compliance facts.
- Confirmed hard-gate failures cannot be silently overridden by AI.
- Source grounding/provenance must be retained where material.
- AI-generated submission narratives/resumes require recruiter review before finalization.
- Autonomous outreach, scheduling, writeback, and other consequential actions require explicit later authorization.

## Consequence

Higher model capability does not by itself authorize more operational control.

Any expansion of AI authority requires policy, audit, rollback, and a superseding ADR or explicit addendum.
